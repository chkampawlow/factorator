EL FATOURA APP MANUAL

Overview
El Fatoura is a business management app for Tunisian small companies. It helps you manage clients, suppliers, products, services, expenses, quotations, sales orders, delivery notes, invoices, credit notes, stock, and PDF extraction.

Main app sections
- Dashboard
- Documents
- Business
- Profile and settings
- Chat assistant


GETTING STARTED

Create an account
1. Open the signup page.
2. Enter your organization name.
3. Enter your fiscal ID.
4. Enter your email and phone number.
5. Create a password.
6. Submit the form and sign in.

First setup after login
1. Open Profile.
2. Fill in your address, website, fax, and phone if they are missing.
3. Check whether your company uses FODEC.
4. Save the profile.
5. Add at least one client.
6. Add products or services before creating invoices.

Navigation
- The top bar contains quick actions, settings, notifications, and account options.
- The left menu contains the main work areas.
- Overview contains the dashboard.
- Documents contains all sell and purchase documents.
- Business contains clients, suppliers, products, services, and expenses.


DASHBOARD

Purpose
The dashboard shows a quick summary of the business.

What you can monitor
- Number of invoices
- Revenue total
- Monthly revenue
- Net result
- Paid and unpaid invoices
- Top client
- Monthly flow
- Best selling items
- Low stock items

How to use it
- Check revenue and unpaid invoices daily.
- Watch low stock items before preparing deliveries.
- Use the sales indicators to know which products move the most.


CLIENTS

Purpose
Clients are the people or companies you sell to.

How to add a client
1. Open Business > Clients.
2. Click Add client.
3. Choose the client type.
4. Enter the name.
5. If it is a company, add the fiscal ID.
6. If it is an individual, add the CIN.
7. Add email, phone, and address if available.
8. Save.

Best practice
- Keep one clean record per real client.
- Add a valid email if you want to send PDFs by email.
- Use the correct fiscal ID or CIN.


SUPPLIERS

Purpose
Suppliers are the people or companies you buy from.

How to add a supplier
1. Open Business > Suppliers.
2. Click Add supplier.
3. Enter the supplier name.
4. Add fiscal ID, phone, email, and address if available.
5. Save.

Why suppliers matter
- They are used for supplier purchase orders.
- They will support future purchase and stock-in flows.


PRODUCTS

Purpose
Products are stock-managed items.

How to add a product
1. Open Business > Products.
2. Click Add product.
3. Enter the product code and name.
4. Set the selling price.
5. Choose the TVA rate.
6. Choose the unit. Default is piece.
7. Enter the starting stock if needed.
8. Save.

Product fields
- Code: internal reference
- Name: product name
- Price: selling price
- TVA rate: tax rate
- Unit: piece, box, kg, etc.
- Stock: quantity available

Stock logic
- Products use movement-based stock.
- Initial stock creates an initial movement.
- Delivery notes reduce stock.
- Future purchase intake should increase stock.

Stock history
Use stock history to see:
- Initial stock
- Delivery movements
- Future stock corrections and stock-in events


SERVICES

Purpose
Services are sellable items that do not use stock.

Examples
- Consulting
- Installation
- Design
- Maintenance

How to add a service
1. Open Business > Services.
2. Click Add service.
3. Enter the code and name.
4. Set the price.
5. Choose the TVA rate.
6. Save.

Difference between products and services
- Products affect stock.
- Services do not affect stock.


EXPENSES

Purpose
Expenses track business spending.

How to add an expense
1. Open Business > Expenses.
2. Click Add expense.
3. Enter the title.
4. Choose a category.
5. Enter the amount.
6. Set the expense date.
7. Add description if needed.
8. Save.

Expense status
- New expenses start as Pending.
- You can later change the status from the list.

Examples of expenses
- Rent
- Internet
- Transport
- Office supplies
- Utilities
- Travel


DOCUMENTS

Recommended sales flow
1. Devis
2. Sales order
3. Delivery note
4. Invoice
5. Credit note if correction is needed

Recommended supplier flow
1. Supplier purchase order
2. Supplier invoice or expense capture
3. Stock-in and purchase follow-up


DEVIS

Purpose
A devis is a quotation sent before the client confirms the sale.

How to create a devis
1. Open Documents > Devis.
2. Select the client.
3. Add products or services.
4. Review the PDF preview.
5. Download or send the PDF.

Important rule
- A devis is not a final invoice.
- A devis should not change stock.
- It is useful to keep devis stored for follow-up.


SALES ORDERS

Purpose
A sales order confirms what the client accepted.

Why it is useful
- It helps organize sales before delivery.
- It acts as an internal commercial confirmation.

How it links to the rest
- A sales order can lead to a delivery note.
- It can also guide the final invoice.


SUPPLIER PURCHASE ORDERS

Purpose
A supplier purchase order is sent to a supplier to request goods or services.

How to create one
1. Open Documents > Purchase orders.
2. Choose the supplier.
3. Set the order date and expected date.
4. Add the requested items.
5. Review the PDF preview.
6. Download the PDF or send it by email to the supplier.
7. Save if you want to keep it in the list.

Why it matters
- It formalizes what you are ordering from the supplier.
- It prepares future purchase and stock operations.


DELIVERY NOTES

Purpose
A delivery note proves that goods left your stock and were delivered.

When to use it
- When physical goods leave stock
- When an order is ready for delivery

Stock effect
- Delivery notes reduce stock for products.
- Services should not reduce stock.

Why it matters
- It links stock operations with billing.


INVOICES

Purpose
Invoices are the official billing documents.

How to create an invoice
1. Open Documents > Invoices.
2. Click Add invoice.
3. Select the client.
4. Add products or services.
5. Review the PDF preview.
6. Create the invoice.
7. Validate the invoice when it is final.

Important rules
- Validation means the invoice is finalized.
- Validation does not mean the invoice is paid.
- Paid and unpaid are payment statuses.
- A validated invoice should no longer be freely edited.

Invoice statuses
- Unpaid
- Paid

Suggested workflow
1. Prepare the invoice.
2. Review the PDF.
3. Validate it when correct.
4. Mark it paid when payment is received.

FODEC
- If the user profile is marked as FODEC, the app includes FODEC where applicable.
- Only enable it if the company really uses it.


CREDIT NOTES / AVOIRS

Purpose
An avoir is a credit note used to correct or reduce an invoice.

Important logic
- An avoir should be created from an existing invoice.
- It should not be created alone without a source invoice.
- Avoir values should be negative.

When to use an avoir
- Return of goods
- Correction of billed amount
- Full or partial reversal of an invoice

Important reminder
- An avoir is not an unpaid invoice.
- It is a correction document.


EXTRACTOR

Purpose
The extractor reads invoice PDFs and tries to extract structured data.

How to use it
1. Open Documents > Extractor.
2. Upload one or many files.
3. Wait for extraction.
4. Review the returned data carefully.

What to verify
- Invoice number
- Issue date
- Due date
- Supplier name
- Client name
- Totals
- TVA
- Currency

Important rule
- Always verify extracted data before using it for accounting or stock decisions.


PROFILE

Purpose
Profile stores your company identity and billing settings.

What to keep updated
- Organization name
- Fiscal ID
- Phone
- Fax
- Address
- Website
- FODEC setting
- 2FA security settings

Why it matters
- Company information appears on generated PDFs.
- Some calculations depend on profile settings.
- Notifications may remind the user to complete missing profile data.

CHAT ASSISTANT

Purpose
The assistant helps users navigate and understand the app.

What it can help with
- Finding the right page
- Explaining the next workflow step
- Answering questions about documents
- Helping with extractor use

Best use
- Ask short, clear questions.
- Use it as guidance, not as final accounting validation.


RECOMMENDED DAILY WORKFLOW

If you sell products
1. Add clients.
2. Add products.
3. Create a devis if needed.
4. Confirm with a sales order if needed.
5. Create a delivery note when goods leave stock.
6. Create and validate the invoice.
7. Mark the invoice as paid when money is received.
8. Use an avoir if a correction is needed.

If you sell services
1. Add clients.
2. Add services.
3. Create a devis if needed.
4. Create the invoice.
5. Validate it.
6. Mark it paid later.

If you buy from suppliers
1. Add suppliers.
2. Create a supplier purchase order.
3. Send the PDF to the supplier.
4. Record the supplier invoice or expense when received.
5. Follow stock-in and purchase logic when available.


GOOD PRACTICES

1. Validate invoices only when they are final.
2. Keep client and supplier records clean.
3. Use products for stock items and services for non-stock work.
4. Check TVA rate before saving any item.
5. Review every PDF before sending it.
6. Verify extractor results before trusting them.
7. Monitor low stock regularly.
8. Keep profile data complete.


TVA QUICK GUIDE

Common TVA rates in the app
- 19% standard rate
- 13% reduced rate
- 7% super reduced rate
- 0% for exempt or zero-rated cases where legally applicable

Reminder
The app calculates the amounts, but the user must still choose the correct legal rate for the real product or service.


COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

1. Creating an avoir without a source invoice.
2. Treating validation as payment.
3. Mixing products and services in the wrong catalog.
4. Forgetting to review the PDF before sending.
5. Using extractor results without checking totals and names.
6. Ignoring low stock warnings.


SHORT GLOSSARY

Devis
Quotation sent before confirmation.

Sales order
Confirmation of what the client accepted.

Supplier purchase order
Order sent to a supplier.

Delivery note
Proof that goods were delivered or left stock.

Invoice
Official billing document.

Avoir
Negative correction document linked to an invoice.

FODEC
Additional tax-related setting used in some Tunisian business cases.

TVA
Value-added tax.


UI DESIGN REFRESH SCOPE

If the app receives a global design refresh to match the Assistant page, the change should follow these rules:

What will change
- Card and panel appearance
- Border radius
- Shadows and glass-like surface styling
- Section header styling
- Button styling
- Input, select, and textarea styling
- Table styling
- Modal styling
- Shared page surface colors in light and dark mode

What will not change
- Page routes
- Page content
- Business logic
- Form fields
- Button actions
- Navigation structure
- Placement of major sections on each page
- Existing workflows

Technical scope
- The refresh should mainly target shared CSS classes used across the app.
- Priority classes include container, dash panels, panel heads, stat cards, buttons, inputs, tables, status pills, and modal surfaces.
- The goal is to make the app visually consistent with the Assistant page without rebuilding each page layout.
