# Depreciation, provision, donation, and subsidy schedules

These optional schedules support the fiscal-result bridge. Every entry records an annual reference, event date, accounting amount, fiscal amount, legal basis, evidence reference, and permanent or temporary treatment.

- Depreciation additionally records gross asset value, opening book value, annual rate, annual charge, and closing book value. The default charge is capped at the opening book value.
- Provisions compare the booked provision expense with the fiscally accepted amount.
- Donations compare the booked donation with the fiscally deductible amount and identify the beneficiary.
- Subsidies compare accounting income recognized with the fiscally taxable amount; their addition/deduction direction is therefore reversed from expense schedules.

The generated additions and deductions support, but do not automatically post into, the fiscal-result reconciliation. Useful lives, rates, deductibility limits, beneficiaries, recognition timing, and evidence must be validated by a Tunisian accountant.
